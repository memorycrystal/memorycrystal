Hosted plans are Free, Pro, Ultra, and Enterprise. Starter is a legacy alias of Pro in the backend. It is not a separate public plan. Unlimited is a backend entitlement with no memory cap. It is not a self-serve checkout plan. Enterprise is contact-only and can be mapped to that entitlement.

Self-hosting is a deployment mode. Hosted plan caps do not apply to a backend you operate unless you enforce them yourself.

## Usage and retention

Your memory allowance counts active memories outside knowledge bases. Archived memories and knowledge-base chunks do not count. See [Usage and allowances](/help/usage-and-allowances) for memory, message, asset, checkpoint, and request caps, and [How Memory Works](/concepts/how-memory-works) for retention and archiving.

Raw uploaded files are not placed in model context. Recall uses derived text, transcripts, summaries, and metadata.

Enterprise lists custom limits, retention, asset storage, multi-seat use, deployment, and dedicated support. Discuss those requirements before choosing a plan.

## Common mistakes

- **Comparing the total active count to your allowance:** that total can include knowledge-base chunks. Use the non-KB usage figure.
- **Treating a support tier as a paid plan:** Full, Capture, Tools, and Hosted describe client capabilities. They are separate from billing.
- **Assuming an upgrade enables injection:** injection remains opt-in on supported hosts. See [Support Tiers](/concepts/support-tiers).
- **Looking for Unlimited at checkout:** it is an entitlement, not a self-serve plan. Contact us for Enterprise requirements.
